What the product is

MazeQuest Studio is a browser-based web app. No install, no API key, no login hassle for the buyer. They pick a theme (Dinosaur, Space, Animals, Christmas), a difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard), and a page count. The app generates mazes, word searches, and dot-to-dot pages, shows a preview of every page, and exports a PDF in US Letter 8.5 x 11 or A4, with an answer key at the back. Every maze is generated by an algorithm, so every one has a working solution.

The buyer is not a marketer. They are a parent who wants a quiet afternoon, a teacher who needs fresh pages for class, or a KDP/Etsy seller who wants new titles fast. That is a wide audience, and it is not the usual WarriorPlus crowd, which is exactly why it converts.
