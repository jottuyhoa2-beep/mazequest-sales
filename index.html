The problem: activity books take forever to make by hand

A good activity book keeps a child busy for an hour. Making one by hand takes days. You have to draw every maze, check every path, lay out every page, and then fix the mistakes.

Buying books is easier, but the same book gets boring fast. Kids finish it, and you buy another one. Teachers face the same thing with a whole classroom: one book, thirty kids, and nothing left to hand out next week.

And if you sell books on KDP or Etsy, hand-drawing each new title is slow work. Slow work means fewer books listed, and fewer books listed means fewer sales.

The fix: an app that builds the book for you

MazeQuest Studio is a simple web app. It does the drawing, the layout, and the checking. You pick the pieces, and it builds a complete book as a PDF you can print or upload.

Every maze it makes has a real, working solution. The app generates the maze with an algorithm, so the path always works. No dead ends that trap a child. No maze with no way out.

What you get

  • Three puzzle types. Mazes, word searches, and dot-to-dot pages. Every page is generated fresh, so no two books come out the same.
  • Four themes kids actually like. Dinosaurs, Space, Animals, and Christmas. Pick one theme for the whole book, and every page matches it.
  • Three difficulty levels. Easy, Medium, and Hard. Match the book to a younger child or an older one, instead of handing them pages that are too easy or too frustrating.
  • You choose the length. Set the number of pages. A short book for a car ride, or a thick one for a rainy week.
  • Preview every page before you print. Look through the whole book on screen first. If a page does not look right, change it before you export.
  • Print-ready PDF, two sizes. Export in US Letter 8.5 x 11 inches or A4. The pages come out at the right size for home printers and for KDP uploads.
  • Answer key included. The last pages of every book hold the solutions, so parents and teachers can check answers fast.
